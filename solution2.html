Our Solution

Qronon is a quantum-inspired machine learning engine that delivers 30–45% more accurate medium- to long-range weather forecasts up to 45 days ahead, using around 10× less compute than traditional physics and AI models.

The problem with today’s forecasts

  • Useful accuracy drops sharply after 7–10 days.
  • Sub-seasonal (15–45 day) forecasts are low-skill and coarse.
  • State-of-the-art NWP and transformer models require huge compute budgets.
  • Uncertainty is often poorly quantified, making risk decisions hard.

The Qronon approach

  • Quantum reservoir–inspired architecture tuned for chaotic dynamics.
  • Probabilistic forecasts 5–45 days ahead, not just 3–5.
  • High resolution in space and time, with efficient ensembles.
  • Designed to plug into existing workflows via APIs and dashboards.

How Qronon works

From raw Earth data to actionable probabilistic forecasts.

1

Ingest global Earth data

We fuse reanalyses, satellite observations, ocean and land-surface data, and existing NWP outputs into a unified, cleaned training stream.

2

Quantum reservoir core

A quantum-inspired reservoir encodes the atmospheric state into a high-dimensional dynamic space, capturing long-range, nonlinear dependencies that drive extremes.

3

Probabilistic ensembles

We roll out fast ensembles to generate full probability distributions, not just single deterministic tracks – ideal for risk and scenario analysis.

4

Sector-specific outputs

Forecasts are transformed into insurance-ready risk indices, grid-relevant energy metrics, and decision-ready insights for public agencies.

Why Qronon is different

Sub-seasonal skill

Designed specifically for the 5–45 day window, where traditional models degrade. Ranked probability skill score (RPSS > 0) against leading baselines on pressure, temperature and wind.

Resource-efficient

Quantum-inspired architecture achieves state-of-the-art accuracy with a fraction of the parameters and compute – enabling larger ensembles, more scenarios, and lower cost per forecast.

Uncertainty-first

Built as a probabilistic engine from day one, delivering calibrated distributions that map naturally into risk, pricing, and portfolio decisions.

High-resolution outputs

From global patterns down to regional and asset-level indicators, suitable for underwriting, grid operations, and operational planning.

Model-agnostic integration

Augments, rather than replaces, existing NWP and AI models – plug Qronon in as a higher-skill, lower-cost sub-seasonal layer.

Quantum-ready

Runs efficiently on classical hardware today, with a clear path to hardware quantum acceleration as platforms mature.
