Why Qronon?

Qronon bridges the power of quantum computing with machine learning for real-world forecasting needs. Our proprietary quantum reservoir computing models outperform classical machine learning in processing high-dimensional, chaotic systems like weather, energy demand, and financial volatility.

  • Extended Forecasting Horizons: Improve the prediction horizon of weather and climate forecasting beyond 10 days, as compared to standard physics or ML based models.
  • AI-Augmented Models: Classical and quantum models are blended with AI tuning to ensure practical usability and interpretability for non-scientists.
  • API & SaaS Integration: Easily integrate forecasts into dashboards or analytics pipelines via RESTful APIs or as a plug-and-play SaaS platform.
  • Scalable Forecasting: Proposed solutions are applicable to risk assessment, finance, logistics, energy markets, and more.
